NEED TO KNOW

Questions before you visit?

What work do you take on?

We handle panel beating, spray painting, restoration, tyres, glass repair and insurance repairs for cars, vans, caravans and campervans.

Can you help with an insurance claim?

Yes. We can guide you through the repair process, assess the damage and coordinate approved work with your insurer.

Do you fit and repair tyres?

Yes. We supply and fit car, van, SUV and 4WD tyres, with balancing, alignment and puncture repairs available.

Where are you located?

You will find us at 470 Marsden Point Road, Ruakaka. Call 09 433 0313 before visiting so we can plan your job.

How long will my repair take?

Timing depends on the damage, parts and paint work required. We will give you a clear estimate after inspecting your vehicle and keep you updated throughout.

Do you provide courtesy vehicles?

Courtesy vehicle options may be available for approved repairs. Ask our team when you book so we can discuss what is available.

Do you work with all insurers?

We can help with insurance repairs and explain the process with your insurer. Bring your claim details and we will help you take the next step.

Do I need to book an inspection?

Booking ahead is recommended so we can set aside time for a proper assessment. Call 09 433 0313 or email us to arrange a visit.
