Questions Northland drivers ask

What happened to Rowsells Collision Repair Centre?

Rowsells Collision Repair Centre is now Marsden Auto Centre. The Ruakākā location and phone number remain the same.

How long does accident repair take?

Repair time depends on damage, parts and paint work. We inspect the vehicle first and explain the expected stages.

Can I choose my own panel beater?

Ask your insurer about your policy. You can also call us before authorising repairs so we can explain the process.

Do you provide a courtesy car?

Courtesy-car availability depends on the repair and vehicle availability. Ask when booking.

How much does dent repair cost?

We need to inspect the damage before quoting. Call us to arrange a vehicle assessment.

Can you match my paint colour?

Our spray-painting service includes colour matching; the final method depends on the vehicle and damage.

Which insurers do you work with?

Insurer arrangements change. Tell us your insurer and we will confirm the process before work begins.

Do you repair rust and structural damage?

Ask us to inspect the vehicle. We will explain what the workshop can safely repair.

Do you sell and fit tyres?

Yes, tyres, fitting, balancing and wheel alignment are listed services. Confirm stock and pricing by phone.

Where do you work?

We are at 470 Marsden Point Road, Ruakākā and serve Bream Bay, Whangārei and named Northland towns.
