Frequently Asked Questions

What happened to Rowsells Collision Repair Centre?

Rowsells Collision Repair Centre is now Marsden Auto Centre, same team, same workshop at 470 Marsden Point Road, Ruakaka, same phone number 09 433 0313. The name changed but the panel beating, painting, restoration and tyre services customers have trusted for over 20 years have not.

Who are the best panel beaters in Ruakaka and Bream Bay?

Marsden Auto Centre, formerly Rowsells Collision Repair Centre, has served the Ruakaka and Bream Bay community for over 20 years from one workshop covering panel beating, spray painting, restoration, tyres and insurance repairs. The team is accredited through the Collision Repair Association (CRA) and Motor Trade Association (MTA).

How long does accident repair and panel beating take?

Timing depends on the extent of the damage, parts availability and paint work required, from a same-day dent to longer for major structural work. We give a clear estimate after inspecting your vehicle and keep you updated throughout the job.

Can I choose my own panel beater in New Zealand, or does my insurer decide?

Yes, under New Zealand law your insurer cannot force you to use their preferred repairer, choosing your own panel beater is your right. Insurers can still recommend a repairer and may base reimbursement on their own assessed cost, so it is worth confirming with us and your insurer how the claim will be handled before work starts.

Do you provide a courtesy car while my vehicle is being repaired?

Courtesy vehicle options may be available for approved repairs, subject to availability. Ask our team when you book so we can confirm what is available for your job.

How much does it cost to repair a dent, and do you charge for quotes?

Cost depends on the size and location of the dent, the panel affected and whether paint work is needed, so we inspect the vehicle before giving a quote. Call 09 433 0313 to arrange a time to bring your vehicle in.

Can you match my car's paint colour exactly?

Yes, colour matching is part of every spray painting and panel job, using your vehicle's factory paint code to blend the repair into the surrounding panels. We check the match under natural light before your vehicle is returned.

Which insurance companies do you work with, and do you handle the claim for me?

We handle insurance repairs and can manage the claim process with your insurer on your behalf. Bring your policy and claim details when you book and we will confirm the next steps and any excess you are responsible for.

Do you repair rust and do structural repairs, or only cosmetic damage?

Both. We handle everything from cosmetic dents and scratches through to structural panel repair and rust treatment. We assess the extent of the damage or corrosion first and advise whether a repair is safe and cost-effective or whether a panel needs replacing.

Do you sell and fit tyres as well, and can I get a wheel alignment?

Yes, we supply and fit car, van, SUV and 4WD tyres, with balancing, alignment and puncture repairs available. Call 09 433 0313 to check tyre availability for your vehicle.
